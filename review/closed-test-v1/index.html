Review on one Android device

  1. Download the PDF QR file above to the test device.
  2. Launch Chekomma. Enter any non-sensitive photo-folder name and choose any visit-team number on the initial setup screen.
  3. For both 출발지 (Start) and 도착지 (End), choose 기타 주소 (Other address), enter 광주광역시 광산구 장덕동 1681, verify the address, and save.
  4. Tap PDF QR 가져오기 (Import QR from PDF), then select the downloaded PDF.
  5. Confirm that eight synthetic destinations appear.
  6. Tap 확인하고 경로 최적화 (Confirm and optimize route).
  7. Choose 오프라인 경로 최적화 (Offline route optimization). This keeps the review route on the device and does not send it to TMAP.

Alternative: display the PNG QR below on a second screen, then tap QR 스캔 (Scan QR) in the app.
